# Dwight Swain's Motivating-Reaction Units (MRUs)

To create immersive, forward-moving narrative rhythm that never feels static or detached, prose must follow the biological law of cause and effect:

## 1. Motivating Unit (Cause)
- An event outside the POV character that stimulates them.
- Rendered objectively from the character's perspective.
- *Rule:* The motivation must be complete before the reaction begins.

## 2. Reaction Unit (Effect)
The character's response must occur in precise psychological sequence:
1. **Feeling (Visceral / Instant):** Immediate sensory or emotional reflex (a chill down the spine, heart skips).
2. **Reflex (Involuntary Action):** Muscle jerk, blink, gasp.
3. **Rational Action & Speech (Conscious Choice):** Words spoken, weapons drawn, planned retreat.

*Editorial Rule:* When an editor or AI diagnoses clunky rhythm, look for inverted MRUs (e.g. speaking before feeling the threat). Quoting suggestions must preserve the author's prose verbatim while pointing out sequencing improvements.
