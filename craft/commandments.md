# The Five Commandments of the Scene (Story Grid Methodology)

A scene is the atomic dramatic quantum of a novel. If a scene does not have all five commandments, it is an incomplete dramatic event.

1. **Inciting Incident:**
   - An event that upsets the status quo of the POV character's life (Causal or Coincidental).
2. **Progressive Complications (Turning Point):**
   - Obstacles and stakes escalate until an active Turning Point occurs—either via an Action (external event) or a Revelation (new information).
3. **Crisis:**
   - The turning point forces a decision. The Crisis must be a true dilemma:
     - *Best Bad Choice:* Two painful options.
     - *Irreconcilable Goods:* Two desirable choices that cannot coexist.
4. **Climax:**
   - The POV character's active decision and physical/emotional execution of that choice.
5. **Resolution:**
   - The immediate dramatic consequence and new status quo established by the climax.
