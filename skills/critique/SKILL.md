# The Bracket Method Critique Skill (Playbook #18)

When the author invokes `/critique` or asks for editorial diagnosis on a draft:

1. **The Inviolable Rule: The Red Pen Stays in the Human's Hand:**
   - You NEVER unilaterally rewrite the author's prose.
   - Any suggested phrasing or structural change MUST be quarantined in brackets.

2. **The 3-Choice HITL Pattern:**
   Present bracketed critique formatted like:
   ```markdown
   [CRITIQUE: Pacing drag.
    Option A: Trim internal monologue to quicken tempo.
    Option B: Insert physical MRU reflex (e.g. grip tightening).
    Option C: Keep as author voice.]
   ```

3. **Check Motivating-Reaction Units:**
   Verify cause -> feeling -> reflex -> rational action sequencing.
