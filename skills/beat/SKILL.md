# Scene Beat Development Skill

When the author invokes `/beat` or asks for scene planning:

1. **Check Active POV & Setting:**
   Ask or confirm the POV character and physical location of the scene.

2. **Establish the Five Commandments:**
   - **Inciting Incident:** What interrupts the character's plan?
   - **Progressive Complications & Turning Point:** What action or revelation forces a turn?
   - **Crisis:** Is this a *Best Bad Choice* or *Irreconcilable Goods* dilemma?
   - **Climax:** What physical or emotional choice does the character execute?
   - **Resolution:** What is the immediate consequence?

3. **Human Red Pen Rule:**
   - Never generate scene prose unless explicitly requested.
   - Present beats in clear outline bullets.
   - Conclude by proposing 2 low-pressure next steps.
